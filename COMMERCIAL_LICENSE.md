## When Do You Need a Commercial License?

You need a commercial license if:

- You use parshred in **proprietary software** that you don't release under AGPL-3.0
- You use parshred in a **network service** (SaaS, API, web app) without open-sourcing the service code
- You want to **modify parshred** without sharing your modifications publicly
- You want to **embed parshred** in a product distributed to customers under a non-AGPL license

You **do NOT** need a commercial license if:

- Your project is open-source under AGPL-3.0 or a compatible license
- You're using parshred for personal or academic research
- You're evaluating parshred for potential commercial use (evaluation is free)

## FAQ

**Q: Can I try parshred before buying a license?**
A: Yes. The AGPL-3.0 version is fully functional. You can evaluate it, prototype with it, and even use it in production for open-source projects indefinitely.

**Q: Does the AGPL apply if I only use parshred internally?**
A: The AGPL's copyleft trigger is "conveying" (distributing) and "remote network interaction" (users interacting over a network). If your use is purely internal with no external users, AGPL may not require you to share code. However, if in doubt, a commercial license removes all ambiguity.

**Q: Can I get a trial commercial license?**
A: Yes, we offer 30-day evaluation licenses for commercial use. Contact us.

**Q: What if I contribute back to parshred?**
A: Contributors retain copyright of their contributions. We may ask for a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) that allows us to offer contributions under both licenses.
