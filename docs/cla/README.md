# Contributor License Agreement

Parshred is dual-licensed under **AGPL-3.0-or-later** and the **Parshred
Commercial License** (see [`../../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md`](../../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md)).
To accept contributions, we require a Contributor License Agreement (CLA)
so we can offer your contribution under both licenses.

## How it works

There's nothing to sign or email. When you open a pull request, the **CLA
Assistant** GitHub bot posts a comment with a link to the
[CLA](CLA.md). Click it, confirm, and you're done. It covers all your future
contributions until you withdraw it.

That's it — no PDFs, no paperwork.

## Why a CLA?

The CLA lets us offer your contribution under both the AGPL open-source
license and the Parshred Commercial License. Without it, the dual-license
model would break.

You retain copyright of your contribution. The CLA is a license, not an
assignment.

not revoke licenses already granted for contributions made before the
