# Contributor License Agreements

Parshred is dual-licensed under **AGPL-3.0-or-later** and the **Parshred
Commercial License** (see [`../../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md`](../../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md)).
To accept contributions, we require a Contributor License Agreement (CLA)
so we can offer your contribution under both licenses.

## Which CLA do I sign?

- **Individuals** contributing on their own behalf: sign the
  [Individual CLA](CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md).
- **Employees contributing on behalf of their employer**: your employer
  signs the [Corporate CLA](CLA-CORPORATE.md).

## How to sign

### Individuals (recommended)

Use the **CLA Assistant** GitHub bot. On your first pull request, the bot
will post a comment with a link to sign. Click it, confirm, and you're
done. The signature is stored against your GitHub identity and covers all
future contributions until you withdraw it.

### Manual signing

If you prefer to sign manually (e.g. for the Corporate CLA):

1. Download the relevant CLA (`CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md` or `CLA-CORPORATE.md`).
2. Fill in the signature block.
3. Email a signed PDF to **licensing@parshred.dev**.

We will record your signature and confirm by reply.

## Why a CLA?

The CLA does two things:

1. It confirms you have the right to contribute the code (you wrote it, or
   your employer authorized you to).
2. It grants us a license to offer your contribution under both the AGPL
   open-source license and the Parshred Commercial License. Without this,
   we could not incorporate community contributions into the commercial
   product, and the dual-license model would break.

You retain copyright of your contribution. The CLA is a license, not an
assignment. (We deliberately chose a license-style CLA over a copyright
assignment CLA.)

## Withdrawal

You may withdraw your CLA at any time by written notice to
licensing@parshred.dev. Withdrawal does not revoke licenses already granted
for contributions made before the withdrawal.
