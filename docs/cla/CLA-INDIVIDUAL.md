# Parshred Individual Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to Parshred! Before we can accept your
contribution, you need to agree to this Individual Contributor License
Agreement ("Agreement"). This is a standard CLA that lets us offer your
contribution under both the AGPL-3.0-or-later open-source license and the
Parshred Commercial License.

## How to Sign

You accept this Agreement by signing in via the CLA Assistant GitHub bot on
your first pull request. The bot will post a link to this document and a
check box to confirm your agreement. You only need to sign once — it covers
all your future contributions to Parshred unless you withdraw it.

If you prefer to sign manually, print this document, fill in the details
below, and email a signed PDF to licensing@parshred.dev.
