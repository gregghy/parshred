# Parshred Corporate Contributor License Agreement

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") is for
contributions made to the Parshred project ("Project") by an employee or
agent of a corporation or other legal entity (the "Corporation"). It
mirrors the Individual CLA but is signed by an authorized representative of
the Corporation and covers all contributions made by the Corporation's
employees, contractors, and agents.

## How to Sign

Email a signed PDF of this document to licensing@parshred.dev. We will
record the Corporation as a signed CLA holder, after which all
contributions from the Corporation's employees and agents are covered.
